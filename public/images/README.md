## Tips
- **Portraits** (leaders) look best shot upright (roughly 3:4), head and shoulders.
- **Gallery** photos can be any size; just number them `1.jpg`, `2.jpg`, ….
- **Leftover old files** in the top level (e.g. `go-*-v4.jpg`,
  `general-overseer-*.jpg`, `telpsam-logo.jpg`, `JITL.jpg`,
  `_plates_tmp/`) are now superseded and safe to delete.
